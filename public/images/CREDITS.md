## Listing photos (`stays/`)

Representative photos for listing cards on the List screen. Each card labels them as representative: they show the kind of place a week is in, never the resort or unit itself, which stay in the unpublished record. Chosen by the listing's country and features; nothing about them reaches the ledger.
